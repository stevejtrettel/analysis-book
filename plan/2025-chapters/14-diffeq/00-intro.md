
We apply the tools of analysis to differential equations. Completeness gives existence via Picard iteration. Matrix exponentials solve linear systems. Fourier methods solve PDEs. And numerical methods require analysis to prove they work.

**Historical Prelude**

- Newton: calculus born from differential equations (motion, gravity)
- 18th-19th century: solving specific equations, cataloguing tricks
- Cauchy, Lipschitz, Picard: existence and uniqueness theory
- Poincaré: qualitative theory when explicit solutions fail
- Today: numerical methods dominate practice, but analysis proves they work
