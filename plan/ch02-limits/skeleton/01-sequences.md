### What Is a Sequence?

::: {#def-sequence}
## Sequence
A sequence is a function $a: \NN \to \RR$. Write $a_n$ for $a(n)$ and $(a_n)$ for the sequence.
:::

### Series and Products as Sequences

::: {#def-series}
## Series
The series $\sum a_n$ is the sequence of partial sums $S_n = a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_n$.
:::

::: {#def-product}
## Infinite Product
The product $\prod a_n$ is the sequence of partial products $P_n = a_1 \cdot a_2 \cdots a_n$.
:::

::: {#prp-geometric-partial-sum}
## Geometric Partial Sum
For $r \neq 1$: $1 + r + r^2 + \cdots + r^n = \frac{1-r^{n+1}}{1-r}$.
:::
